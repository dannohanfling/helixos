Common questions

How do I sign in?

Sign in with your email and the password you set when you joined. Forgot it? Use Forgot your password on the sign-in page and we will email you a reset link. New members join with the invite from their coach. Still stuck? Email us from your account address.

How do streaks and the fire work?

Any day you show up counts: opening HelixOS, locking in, closing out, or earning points. Weekends count too.

  • On weekdays your streak bonus grows through the week: 10, 20, 40, 80, then 160 points. It starts again each Monday.
  • Saturday and Sunday are worth +40 each.
  • Your fire grows one stage each day, from Ember to Beacon.
  • If you miss a day, the fire goes back to an ember and the streak starts again. Your days in the arena never reset.
How do I earn points and ranks?

Points come from the work that grows your business. Some examples: locking in your day (+10), closing it out (+20), publishing a post (+15), starting a DM conversation (+5), and booking a call (+25), plus your streak bonus.

The ranks are Artisan (0), Philosopher (100), Sage (500), Alchemist (2,000), Sentinel (5,000), Centurion (12,000), Oracle (25,000), Titan (50,000) and Olympian (100,000). A rank you have reached is yours to keep. Your rank sets your seat in the Colosseum.

If your points look wrong, tell us through I have an issue or a suggestion and your coach will check them.

How do I connect GoHighLevel or Airtable?

Open Settings → Connect tools in HelixOS and follow the steps for that tool. For GoHighLevel, you create a private integration in your own GoHighLevel account, tick the permissions shown, and paste the key into HelixOS. Your key is stored encrypted, and you can disconnect it at any time.

What is my Board, and is it a real person?

Your Board is a panel of AI advisors that answers your business questions from different angles. The advisors are AI, not real people, including the "style" seats and the Danno seat. You always decide. Read AI and your Board.

How do I change notifications?

Go to Settings in HelixOS to choose your morning and evening reminder times and which emails you get. In the phone app you can also turn notifications off in your phone's settings.

Who can see my HumanOS health data?

Only you, unless you turn on Share with my coach in HumanOS. That shares a summary only, never your detailed log. Other members never see it. See the Privacy Policy.

Who can see me in the Colosseum?

Other members of your coaching workspace can see your display name, rank, points and wins. Your photo shows only if you turn it on. See the Community Guidelines.

How do I export or delete my data?

Use Download my data in Settings to get a copy of everything you've put into HelixOS. To delete your account, or only your HumanOS data, see Delete your account.

Reporting a problem with someone

If a member's behaviour breaks our Community Guidelines, tell us through I have an issue or a suggestion or by email. We review reports within 24 hours.

Security issues

If you think you've found a security problem in HelixOS, email danno@evolveomega.com with the subject "HelixOS security". Please don't test it on other members' accounts.
