Community Guidelines

Last updated 18 October 2026

HelixOS has shared spaces, such as the Colosseum, where members see each other's ranks, points and wins. These guidelines keep those spaces encouraging and safe. They apply wherever other members can see you.

What we ask

Celebrate honestly

Share real wins and real numbers. Don't fake activity, inflate results or game points. It only cheats you, and points can be corrected.

Lift each other up

Be kind and direct. Disagree with ideas, not people. Everyone here is building something.

Protect privacy

Don't share another member's information, or your clients' personal details, without permission. What members share in HelixOS stays in HelixOS.

No selling to members

Don't pitch, recruit or send promotions to other members unless they ask for it.

Keep it safe

We don't allow:

  • harassment, threats or bullying;
  • hate speech or discrimination;
  • sexual or violent content;
  • impersonation;
  • scams;
  • anything illegal.

We have zero tolerance for abusive or objectionable posts. A win that breaks these guidelines is removed, and so may be the account that posted it.

Your photo is your choice

Your photo shows in the Colosseum only if you turn it on. Use a photo of yourself, never of someone else.

Reporting

If something breaks these guidelines, tap Report on the win or the member in the Colosseum, or tell us through I have an issue or a suggestion in the HelixOS menu, or at danno@evolveomega.com. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours. We won't tell the other member who reported them.

You can also Block a member. You won't see their wins, their name or their photo, and they won't be told. Unblock them any time in Settings.

What happens next

Depending on what happened, we may:

  • remove content;
  • adjust points;
  • give a warning;
  • pause community features for an account;
  • end access under our Terms.

Serious or illegal behaviour may be removed straight away and reported to the authorities.
