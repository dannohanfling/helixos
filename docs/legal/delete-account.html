Delete your account

Last updated 16 October 2026

You can delete your HelixOS account at any time. You can also delete only your HumanOS health data and keep the rest.

Delete in the app

  1. Open HelixOS and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete my account, or Delete my HumanOS data to remove only your health data.
  3. Confirm. If you'd like a copy of your data first, use Download my data in Settings before you confirm.

Or ask us by email

Email danno@evolveomega.com with the subject Account Deletion Request, from the email address on your account. We will confirm it is you, then delete your account.

Email a deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your profile and sign-in link to HelixOS.
  • Your work in HelixOS: lock-ins and close-outs, tasks, offers, avatars, ladders, logged DMs and calls, Board questions and answers, check-ins, and your points, ranks and streaks.
  • All HumanOS health data.
  • The keys for every tool you connected. We delete our copy. To fully revoke a key, you can also delete it in that tool.
  • Your push notification tokens, and your place in the Colosseum and other community spaces.

Timing, and what we keep

  • We delete your data within 30 days of your request. Copies in our backups are removed in our normal backup cycle.
  • We may keep a small amount where the law requires it, or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. We also keep statistics that don't identify you.
Deleting HelixOS does not cancel your coaching program or delete your Evolve Omega community or GoHighLevel account. To change those, contact us.
