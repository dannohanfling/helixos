AI and your Board

Last updated 16 October 2026

HelixOS uses AI to help you think, plan and act. Here's what that means, so you can use it well.

Your Board is AI

Your Board is a council of AI advisors. Each seat looks at your question from a different angle: strategy, operations, your customer, risk, and so on. Together they give a recommendation and one to three next steps that fit the hours you have that week.

  • The Danno seat is guided by Danno's teaching and frameworks. It is AI, not Danno answering live. For Danno himself, bring it to your coaching call.
  • "Style" seats, such as "{Name} style", are AI personas inspired by that person's publicly available work. They are not that person, don't speak for them, and aren't affiliated with or endorsed by them. They never put words in that person's mouth or quote them as if live.
  • Custom seats you create are yours and visible only to you and your coach.
  • Voices: if spoken answers are added, AI voices will never imitate a real person.

You decide

AI can be confident and wrong. It can miss context, misread numbers or be out of date.

  • Treat answers as input from advisors, not instructions.
  • Check anything that matters, especially money, legal, tax, health or anything to do with your clients' information.
  • AI answers are not professional advice. See our Terms.

What the AI sees

  • To answer you, HelixOS sends the AI provider your question and the relevant parts of your workspace, such as your offers, numbers and recent activity.
  • Your HumanOS health data is included only when you ask about it.
  • If you connect your own AI key, the request runs on your account with that provider.
  • Where a provider lets us choose, we pick settings that stop it from using your content to train its models.
  • Your Board history is saved, so check-ins and follow-ups can refer back to it. You can delete it with your account.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

Other AI features

AI also helps with drafts, such as posts, comment ladders and DM replies, and with suggestions and summaries. Read and edit anything before you send or publish it. You are responsible for what you post under your name.

Something wrong?

If an answer is wrong, harmful or off-brand, tell us through I have an issue or a suggestion in the menu, or at danno@evolveomega.com. We use these reports to improve the Board.
