Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

This policy explains what HelixOS collects, why, who sees it, and the choices you have. HelixOS is a coaching workspace for small service businesses, run by Eye To Eye Media LLC, which does business as Evolve Omega ("we", "us"). It covers the web app at helixos.evolveomega.com and the HelixOS apps for iPhone and Android.

The short version. We use your information to run HelixOS for you and your coach. We do not sell it, we do not use it for advertising, and your HumanOS health data is visible only to you. You can export or delete your data at any time.

1. What we collect

Account and sign-in

Your name, email address and business name, the invite code you joined with, and your password. We store your password only as a secure one-way hash, so we cannot read it.

Your work in HelixOS

What you create and record in the app, such as daily lock-ins and close-outs, tasks, offers, client avatars, content and comment ladders, the DMs and calls you log, your Board questions and answers, check-ins, and your points, ranks and streaks.

A reel video, only if you attach it to a post. It is kept in private storage that only you and your coach can open, and it goes to your connected social planner when you publish that post.

Connected tools

If you connect a tool such as GoHighLevel, Airtable or an AI provider, we store the access key you give us (encrypted) and read the data that tool shares, but only for the features you turn on. For example, we read counts of new conversations and booked calls to show your pipeline in the Aqueduct.

Community

Your display name, rank, points and wins, which appear to other members of your coaching workspace in places like the Colosseum. Your profile photo appears only if you choose to show it.

Device and usage

Your device type, operating system, app version, language, a device token for push notifications, the screens you open and when, and technical logs when something goes wrong.

Support

The messages, issues and ideas you send us, and our replies.

We do not collect your precise location, your contacts, or payment card details in HelixOS.

2. HumanOS health data

HumanOS is the optional health side of HelixOS. If you use it, you may record food and meals, body weight, exercise, sleep, habits, medications and notes.

  • Only you see it, unless you choose to share a summary. Other members never see it. Your coach sees nothing unless you turn on Share with my coach in HumanOS. That shares a summary only: days in your target range, your last weigh-in, sessions and goals. Your detailed health log is never shared.
  • It is used only to show you your own tracking, for example your trends, your week and your shopping list.
  • It is never used for advertising, never sold, and never shared with your connected business tools.
  • We send it to a third party only when you ask. For example, when you send a shopping list to Instacart, or ask an AI feature about your own HumanOS entries.
  • You can delete it at any time without deleting the rest of your account.

HumanOS is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. See our Terms.

3. How we use it

  • To run HelixOS: sign you in, save your work, keep your streaks and points, and show your dashboards.
  • To support your coaching, so your coach can see your progress and help you. HumanOS is part of this only if you share a summary.
  • To power the features you use, such as your Board, the Aqueduct, ladders and imports.
  • To send the notifications and emails you have switched on.
  • To answer support requests and fix problems.
  • To keep HelixOS secure and prevent abuse.
  • To understand which features help members, using usage data in aggregate, so we can improve them.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on our agreement with you, our legitimate interest in running and improving HelixOS, your consent (for example for HumanOS and push notifications), or a legal obligation.

4. AI features

Some features use AI models, including your Board, suggestions and drafts. When you use one, we send the AI provider what that request needs, such as your question and the relevant parts of your HelixOS workspace.

  • When you connect your own AI key, the request runs on your account with that provider and is covered by their terms and privacy policy.
  • Where a provider lets us choose, we pick settings that stop it from using your content to train its models.
  • Writing out a reel happens on your device. When you choose a reel in New from a reel, the listening model runs in your browser. Your video and its sound are not uploaded for that, to us or to anyone. The video is uploaded only if you attach it to a post. The words it writes out are sent with your request when you press Create post, like any other AI feature.
  • AI answers can be wrong. You decide what to act on. Read more in AI and your Board.

5. Who can see it

  • Your coach (Evolve Omega coaches and team members working with you) can see your work in HelixOS to support you. They do not see your HumanOS data, except the summary you choose to share.
  • Other members of your coaching workspace see only your community information: display name, rank, points, wins, and your photo if you opt in.
  • Service providers who run HelixOS for us can use it only to provide their service to us: hosting (Vercel), our database host, email delivery, Apple and Google push notification services, and AI model providers.
  • Tools you connect or send to, such as GoHighLevel, Airtable or Instacart, receive what you choose to send them.
  • When the law requires it: to comply with the law or a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our members, the public or us.
  • If our business changes hands: in a merger, acquisition or transfer of assets, your information may move as part of that transaction, under this policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for targeted or cross-context advertising.

6. Connected tools

You choose which tools to connect, and you can disconnect them at any time in HelixOS. When you disconnect a tool, we delete the key you gave us for it. Data that tool already sent to HelixOS stays part of your workspace until you delete it. Your use of each tool is governed by its own terms and privacy policy. Any usage charges on your own accounts, such as AI usage on your own key, are between you and that provider.

7. Notifications and email

You choose your reminders and emails in HelixOS under Settings. You can also turn off push notifications in your phone's settings. Service messages about your account, such as security notices, may still be sent.

8. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active.

When you delete your account, we delete your personal information within 30 days. Copies in our backups are removed in our normal backup cycle.

We may keep a small amount for longer where the law requires it, or to resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. We also keep statistics with nothing that identifies you.

9. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: view and edit your information in HelixOS.
  • Export: download a copy of your data at any time with Download my data in Settings, or ask us.
  • Delete: delete your account, or only your HumanOS data. See Delete your account.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off HumanOS, notifications or connected tools at any time.
  • More rights where you live: depending on your state or country (for example California, or the EU and UK), you may have further rights. These include knowing what we hold, objecting to or limiting some processing, and not being treated differently for using your rights.

To make a request, email danno@evolveomega.com from the address on your account. We will confirm it is you and reply within the time the law requires, usually 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

10. Security

We protect your information with encryption in transit, encrypted storage of the keys you connect, access controls that keep each member's data separate, and activity logs. No system is perfectly secure.

We will never ask for your password or your keys by email, text or phone. If something looks wrong, tell us straight away.

11. Children

HelixOS is for adults running a business and is not for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Where data is stored

We are based in the United States, and HelixOS data is processed and stored in the United States. If you use HelixOS from another country, your information will be transferred to the United States, where data protection laws may differ from yours.

13. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in HelixOS or by email before it takes effect.
